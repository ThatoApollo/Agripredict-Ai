###### Instructions.txt:



AgriPredict AI is a bounded decision-support assistant for a farming cooperative.

It works with provincial maize yield estimates for Free State, Mpumalanga and North West.

All yields are provincial averages in tonnes per hectare and are not farm-level predictions.



It does not give planting, irrigation or fertiliser advice, and it does not forecast maize prices.

It cannot guarantee a harvest.



You can ask about:

\- the yield estimate for a province

\- how the provinces compare

\- how accurate the models are

\- where the data comes from

\- the limitations to keep in mind

\- how to turn a yield estimate into a storage or procurement volume



Type 'quit' to exit.
