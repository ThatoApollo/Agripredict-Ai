# Annual Yield Analysis

The chart in `annual_yield_trends.png` compares mean and median maize yields in the sampled Kenyan fields from 2016–2020. Mean yield rose in 2017, declined in 2018 and 2019, and recovered slightly in 2020. The median stayed below the mean each year, consistent with higher-yield observations pulling the average upward.

Different fields may have been sampled each year, so the chart does not track the same farms or represent national averages. It describes historical variation and does not predict future yields.
